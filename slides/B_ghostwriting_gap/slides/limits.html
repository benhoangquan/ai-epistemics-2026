What this does not show

Synthetic disputes

Written by a model, 78 ambiguous scenarios, no real people.

LLM judges only

Two judges and two Claude Haiku runs. No human labels yet. One judge also wrote the pilot scenarios.

Prompt-dependent

The mediator prompt cuts bias by 20 to 44 points. Other prompts may give other results.

A drafted message is advocacy by nature. The claim is that drafts diverge from the same model’s own verdicts, not that every one-sided draft is a failure.

A human-labelled pack of 120 drafts was prepared but not labelled. The pilot’s 83 percent added-fact rate came from a checker bug; with it fixed, strict tellings are flagged 3 percent of the time after one regeneration.
