Method · the mirror test

Tell the same dispute from each side

1 · FACT SHEET

One neutral dispute

Landlord and tenant, employer and employee, ex-partners, neighbours.

2 · TWO TELLINGS

First person, each party

Same facts, each narrator emphasizes their own side.

3 · MIRROR BIAS

Add the two ratings, minus 100

0 is symmetric. Positive means the model sides with whoever is narrating.

78 ambiguous disputes, 4 drafters, 2 LLM judges. We compare each model’s verdict with its own draft.

Gemma-4-31b-it, gemma-4-26b-a4b-it, glm-5.3-flash and qwen3.8-27b were the drafters. The two LLM judges were deepseek-v4-flash, which also wrote the pilot scenarios, and gemma-4-31b-it, which is also a drafter. The disputes are synthetic.
