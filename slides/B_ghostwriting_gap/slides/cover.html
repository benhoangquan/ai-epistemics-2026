Verdict Tests Miss It: LLM Ghostwriting Takes the Narrator’s Side

In a verdict, models do not side with the narrator. In the message they draft for you, they often do.

Quan Tran · Alexandre Tancrède · Anas Elghoudane · Pierre Duhoux

Equal contribution · University of Montreal · Quan Tran also Mila – Quebec AI Institute

The question: people already ask chatbots to write the message they will send in a dispute. Do the chatbots take the sender’s side? Existing tests ask for a verdict. We also ask the model to draft the message. Paper and code are at github.com/benhoangquan/ai-epistemics-2026.
