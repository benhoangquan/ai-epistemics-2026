The problem

A source with no information can still choose what to claim.

Which events

Claim only the near-certain ones.

How many

Claim a handful, or flood.

What probability

Copy the crowd, or extremize.

Reliability scores rank sources by the claims they chose. If a source can learn that choice, how far up can it climb?

Leaderboards and track records are only trusted if they resist gaming. A source can pick easy events, claim few or many, and shade its probabilities. The known part is that Brier-scored platforms reward selective choice (Sempere and Lawsen, 2021). The open question is how far a source that learns the choice can climb under each scoring rule.
