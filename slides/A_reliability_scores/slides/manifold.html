Accuracy rankings reward a habit of backing favourites

LEDGER

168,701

dated, resolved claims from 28,704 users. We analyse 83,298 claims from 1,855 users with at least 20 claims.

GAMING SIGNATURE

ρ 0.42

[0.38, 0.45]: share of bets on 90%+ favourites against accuracy rank minus information-gain rank.

Crowd-relative rules are too noisy to rank individuals here: split-half ρ is about 0.07 at 20 claims or more.

Both accuracy and the favourite share are oriented by the side of the bet. Reading accuracy from the post-bet price would halve the signature to 0.22. A bootstrap over markets gives [0.32, 0.39]. Play money, no ground-truth skill, and small bets move the market price very little, so crowd-relative scores partly measure bet size. The signature is correlational, not proof of intent.
