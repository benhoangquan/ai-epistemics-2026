Which Reliability Score Survives a Learning Cheater?

A zero-skill source that learns which claims to make, tested against 16 scoring rules.

Quan Tran · Alexandre Tancrède · Anas Elghoudane · Pierre Duhoux

Equal contribution · University of Montreal · Quan Tran also Mila – Quebec AI Institute

Our question: platforms rank sources by their track record. What if a source with no skill can learn which claims to make so the ranking puts it near the top? We trained that source against 16 scoring rules. Paper and code are at github.com/benhoangquan/ai-epistemics-2026.
