Method

Train a zero-skill cheater against each rule

WORLD

3,000 events

5 topics, 200 honest sources with skill from 0.1 to 0.9. The crowd prior is calibrated.

CHEATER

14 parameters

Event choice, report map and volume, trained with the cross-entropy method. It sees only the crowd prior and topic.

SCORE

Its percentile

Rank among the 200 honest sources on fresh worlds. 50 is the median honest source.

16 rules, 30 seeds, compared with 10 hand-coded cheats.

The cheater is a zero-skill policy: it has no private information. We reward it with its percentile among 200 honest sources and report held-out percentiles on 20 fresh worlds per seed, with 95 percent bootstrap intervals over seeds. 13 rules were in the original audit and 3 were added after review: two peer scores and a fixed-effects Brier.
