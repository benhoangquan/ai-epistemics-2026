On real Manifold data the recalibrated baseline scored worse than the raw price: −0.832 against −0.790 mean log score.

The simulated fix assumes a stationary bias. On Manifold the fitted map drifts across time blocks and question horizons shrink from about 180 days to about 18. We also found a look-ahead leak in our backtest: 9 percent of block-3 fit markets resolved after the block started. The leak favours recalibration, so it does not explain the negative result. We make no claim that recalibration improves real leaderboards.
