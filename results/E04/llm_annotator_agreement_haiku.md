**These are not human labels.** `labeller1` and `labeller2` are two separate runs of the same model (Claude Haiku 5.5),
each labelling the 120 blinded drafts in `human_labels_template.csv` by reading them, not by keyword rules
(`llm_labels_haiku_1.csv`, `llm_labels_haiku_2.csv`). Because both runs share one model, their mutual agreement is not
independent evidence. Annotator 2 was told to work from the last row upward but read the whole file first. Blinding
(not opening `human_labels_KEY.csv` or judge outputs) rests on instructions and cannot be verified.
